The Philosophy of Remeslo

اَلْعِلْمُ نُورٌ
"Knowledge is light"— carved above the gates of Building 67

Remeslo (Russian for "craft") was founded on one radical idea from the old Samarkand bazaars: you do not study a trade, you are apprenticed to it. No lecture halls full of sleeping students — every class is a workshop, every professor is a master, and every student leaves with something they made with their own hands.

The whole campus is shaped like the number 67 — not because it means anything, but because the founder saw it in a dream next to a plate of plov and decided that was a sign. The "6" holds the kitchens and forges; the "7" holds the labs and lecture domes; the bridge between them is the Grand Hall with the eternal kazan.

Tuition is free. The catch: an exam every single day at 09:00. Pass, and you keep your Mini Cooper, your diamond stipend and your seat at the plov table. Fail three days in a row, and you are sent to defeat the recruitment boss Brymdyshch to earn your way back in.

Campus Life

The Grand Hall is the beating heart of Remeslo: carpets from every region of the Silk Road, a ceiling dripping with churchkhela garlands, and walls literally set with diamonds (do not pry them off — the boss Brymdyshch counts them nightly).

Dorms are in the two towers. The left tower smells permanently of cumin and warm bread; the right tower hums with servers and the click of mechanical keyboards. Everyone agrees the left tower has the better parties.

Clubs include the Kazan Society, the SIUUU Sprint Team, competitive Samsa-folding, and a secretive math circle that meets inside the glowing "67" sign after dark. Rumour: members solve one impossible problem per semester and are rewarded in raw diamonds.

The unofficial motto whispered between students: "survive the 09:00, and the rest of the day is a feast."

A Mini Cooper for every student

Keys are handed over together with your student ID. Yes, there is an exam every single day — but you drive to class in your own Mini. A fair deal by Samarkand standards.

Insider Facts

Things they don't put in the brochure.

🕌The "6" faces Mecca, the "7" faces the sunset — so the whole campus glows gold twice a day and smells of bread once.
🍚The kazan has never been cleaned. Chefs insist the 40-year crust is the flavor. Health inspectors were fed plov and left smiling.
💎Your diamond stipend is real — but the campus jeweler buys them back at a "friendly" rate. Somehow everyone stays broke by Friday.
🚗All Mini Coopers are the same shade of pomegranate red. Lose yours in the parking lot and it becomes a genuine AMCS search-algorithm exam.
📝The 09:00 exam has one legendary question that reappears every leap year: "How much plov is enough?" The only correct answer is "more".
👹Brymdyshch isn't evil — he's just really, really good at recruitment quotas. Beat him once and he personally hand-delivers your acceptance letter.
🍫MrBeast's "Glass Wool & Chocolate" lab is the highest-rated elective. Nobody remembers what glass wool has to do with anything, but the chocolate is unlimited.
